Why we need it now

Our community has outgrown its space.

For more than 40 years, Masjid Al Furqan has served West Cobb. Today, families from more than 40 countries call this masjid home, but our building has not grown with us.

We hold two Jumu’ah prayers because one cannot fit the community. On Eid, the parking lot fills before prayer begins, and families worship in the pavilion or outside on the concrete.

Our classrooms are full. Our programs are growing. Children are waiting for a place to learn.

What your support makes possible

More than a building.
A place to belong.

This center is designed around the real needs of our community, from the first prayer of the day to the classes, conversations, and acts of service that follow.

01

Dignified space to worship

Expanded prayer space will welcome the whole community for Jumu’ah and Eid, indoors, together, with comfort and dignity.

02

Room to learn

Modern classrooms for Hifdh, Arabic, Islamic studies, Sunday school, kindergarten, and after school programs, so we can welcome more children instead of turning them away.

◯
03

Space for youth to thrive

A full size basketball court, indoor gym, women’s gym, and dedicated areas for youth programs and Boy Scouts, keeping the next generation active and connected to the masjid.

☕
04

Connections that last

A welcoming café and lounge where youth, elders, new Muslims, families, and young professionals can share a cup of coffee and build real community.

♡
05

Care for every family

Dedicated, accessible space for special needs programming at our MUHSEN certified masjid, with an elevator and accessibility throughout the building.

✦
06

Guidance and service

An Imam’s office for nasiha and counseling, a clinic, and dignified rooms to welcome interfaith, civic, and community leaders.

◇
07

Support in every season

A kitchen and funeral services office to strengthen our monthly food pantry, serving people of all faiths, and support families through janazah services.
